I am Zhe (Monica) Zhong, Ph.D., a researcher in bioengineering and mechanical engineering focused on immunology, biomaterials, and tissue engineering.

My work centers on engineering human immune organoid platforms to study B-cell and T-cell responses in health and disease, with applications in vaccine evaluation and immune profiling. I completed my Ph.D. at the Georgia Institute of Technology (2025), after beginning doctoral training at Cornell University.

I have authored publications in *Nature Materials*, *Science Advances*, *ACS Central Science*, *Advanced Materials*, and related journals, and I am a co-inventor on multiple patents in immune tissue systems and antibody technologies.
